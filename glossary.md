# Glossaries & Definitions

Basic, simple definitions for GIS concepts and terms can be found in different glossaries and dictionaries:

* [GIS Dictionary](https://support.esri.com/en-us/gis-dictionary) from Esri  
* [GIS Dictionary – Geospatial Definition Glossary](https://gisgeography.com/gis-dictionary-definition-glossary/) from [GISGeography.com](http://GISGeography.com)  
* [Definition List](https://gistbok-ltb.ucgis.org/page/current/abbreviation/list) from the UCGIS GIS\&T BoK (University Consortium for Geographic Information Science Geographic Information Science & Technologies Body of Knowledge)

For more detailed descriptions of GIS concepts, encyclopedias and other reference works are useful:

* [Body of Knowledge (BoK)](https://gistbok-ltb.ucgis.org/) from the UCGIS Geographic Information Science & Technologies (GIS\&T)
